How this is wired
  • Every fly is a real variant on catalog.theflyshop.com, pulled from the public storefront. Prices, stock and photos are the shop's own.
  • Flow is live from USGS gauge 11370500 at Keswick. Weather is live for Redding. Both fall back to the report's last reading if a feed is down.
  • The pack button opens a Shopify cart permalink with every fly in it, tagged so the order shows it came from this report.
  • The guide's notes are the shop's September 1 text, shown pending permission.
